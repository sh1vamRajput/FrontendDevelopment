The Art of Readable Typography

Typography is not just about choosing a font — it’s about how text feels on a page. The right combination of line-height, letter-spacing, and font-weight can make a huge difference in user experience.

In modern design, readability is king. Subtle spacing and visual rhythm help readers stay engaged. For instance, sans-serif fonts like Inter or DM Sans look clean on screens, while serif fonts like Merriweather feel warm and traditional.

Good Typography = Better Communication

“Typography is the craft of endowing human language with a durable visual form.”
— Robert Bringhurst

A well-designed page uses hierarchy wisely. Headings should stand out, but not shout. Paragraphs should breathe, not suffocate. Even small details like highlighted text or emphasized words can guide the reader’s focus.
