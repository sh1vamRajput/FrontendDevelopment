Welcome to Kyoto

Kyoto is Japan’s ancient capital, filled with breathtaking temples, vibrant gardens, and timeless traditions. It blends the old and the new — from centuries-old shrines to modern cafes and cultural festivals.

Rich Cultural Heritage

Experience tea ceremonies, elegant kimonos, and the charm of geisha districts like Gion. Kyoto preserves the art and culture of old Japan like no other city.

Traditional Kyoto Cuisine

Kyoto’s food scene offers delicate kaiseki meals, matcha desserts, and street foods that capture the city’s peaceful spirit. Every bite tells a story of refinement and balance.

Famous Attractions

Visit Fushimi Inari Shrine’s red torii gates, Kinkaku-ji’s golden pavilion, and Arashiyama’s serene bamboo forest — each site a masterpiece of Japanese culture and beauty.
